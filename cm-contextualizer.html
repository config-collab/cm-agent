🔒 Privacy Notice
The Contextualizer uses the LLM to extract decision provenance from your documents — who decided what, why, what alternatives were rejected. This means your document content is sent to the LLM provider you choose.

Ollama (local): Runs on your machine. Nothing leaves your network. Recommended for defense, aerospace, nuclear, or any confidential IP.
Claude / OpenAI: Data is sent to their servers. Review their data processing policies before use.
